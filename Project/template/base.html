A Structured Financing Process

ADX organizes financing activities into a clear, traceable workflow from customer onboarding to repayment.

STEP 01

Customer Onboarding & Application

Authorized representatives capture customer information and create financing applications based on the customer's needs.

  • Customer registration and profile management.
  • Financing application creation.
  • Document upload and application tracking.
STEP 02

Verification & Review

Applications move through verification and review before a financing decision is made.

  • Document review.
  • Verification checklist.
  • GPS and timestamp evidence where applicable.
  • Verification notes and recommendations.
STEP 03

Approval, Payment & Monitoring

Verified applications are reviewed for approval, after which approved transactions can be tracked through payment and subsequent portfolio activities.

  • Application approval or decline.
  • Supplier and payment information.
  • Payment confirmation and receipts.
STEP 04

Repayment & Customer Lifecycle

Financing does not end with disbursement. ADX maintains customer and transaction history to support repayment tracking and future financing decisions.

  • Repayment recording.
  • Outstanding financing monitoring.
  • Customer lifecycle tracking.
  • Reapplication for existing customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about ADX and the financing management process.

What is Arete DeltaX (ADX)?

ADX is a registered corporation working to support growth in agricultural value chains and markets through financing and related business support.

What type of financing does ADX support?

The platform is designed to support credit and inventory financing activities, including application processing, verification, approval, payment and repayment tracking.

How is a financing application processed?

Applications move through customer onboarding, document submission, verification, review, approval, payment and subsequent repayment monitoring.

Does the system support verification?

Yes. Authorized verification personnel can review customer information, documents, checklists, verification notes and supporting evidence before applications proceed.

Can existing customers apply again?

Yes. The system is designed to maintain customer history and support reapplication for existing customers, subject to the applicable financing process.
